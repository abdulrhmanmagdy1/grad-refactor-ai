# Supervisor reviews

Hold a review after P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5. Each review has an Issue linked to its milestone. No appointment or academic approval is implied by the existence of a task.

## Prepared review packs

Each pack follows the [review template](review-template.md). It holds the phase outcomes, links to evidence, open problems and the decisions the team asks for. Its feedback fields stay empty until the meeting. None of these meetings had taken place when the packs were prepared, and no supervisor feedback is recorded in them.

| Review | Pack | Main decisions requested |
|---|---|---|
| R1, P1 Scope and feasibility | [r1-scope-and-feasibility.md](r1-scope-and-feasibility.md) | ADR12 task choice, model and resources, academic scope, title wording, coding-agent assistance, logistics |
| R2, P2 Data and baseline | [r2-data-and-baseline.md](r2-data-and-baseline.md) | License of the team-authored data, dataset design, the refactor-v2 prompt change, the re-freeze history, the protocol, oracle feedback, generation limits |
| R3, P3 Integration and first training run | [r3-integration-and-training.md](r3-integration-and-training.md) | Compute on Kaggle, hardware for the test evaluation, project data on Kaggle, real-model end-to-end evidence, the fine-tuning deliverable |
| R4, P4 Integrated first release | [r4-first-release.md](r4-first-release.md) | Feature freeze, runner safety level, delivered model configuration, protocol freeze and targets, acceptance evidence |
| R5, P5 Initial delivery | [r5-delivery.md](r5-delivery.md) | Reading of the results, release content and visibility, reproduction evidence, report and declaration, next priorities |

The demonstration for R3 to R5 follows the [demonstration script](../delivery/demo-script.md), and the presentation follows the [presentation outline](../delivery/presentation-outline.md).

## Before the meeting

Prepare a short summary, links to outputs, a demonstration where available, actual results, open problems, alternatives, and specific decisions needed. Arrange the review before the phase ends. No supervisor messages or calendar invitations are sent automatically.

## After the meeting

Copy the review template, record the date and attendees, identify the exact version shown, and record feedback, agreed decisions, and pending questions. Create linked follow-up Issues. Distinguish a team proposal from a supervisor decision. Continue independent work while waiting; hold decisions that change scope or evaluation until agreement.
