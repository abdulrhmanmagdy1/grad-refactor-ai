# enrollment

An online course platform. Courses have a seat limit, an instructor and prerequisites;
students enroll until the course is full and then join a first-come-first-served
waitlist. Students work through lessons and take quizzes (a quiz is passed at 70% of the
points). A student who finishes every lesson and passes every quiz of a course gets a
certificate, and the course then counts as completed for prerequisite checks.
